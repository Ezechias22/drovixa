## Data Drovixa processes

Drovixa may process account details, authentication identifiers, profiles,
device/session data, watch progress and history, saved content, comments,
reports, rewards/referrals, purchases and entitlements, notification tokens,
support messages, approximate country, security logs, and crash/performance
telemetry. Payment card details must remain with the approved payment/store
provider and must not be stored by Drovixa.

## Purposes

Data is used to provide streaming and account features, synchronize progress,
enforce content access and device limits, deliver notifications, prevent abuse,
process entitlements, support users, improve reliability, and comply with law.
