# Drovixa Community Guidelines

Users may discuss stories and creators respectfully. The following are not
allowed: threats, harassment, hateful conduct, sexual exploitation, child
sexual abuse material, doxxing, spam, scams, impersonation, coordinated abuse,
malware, piracy links, copyright infringement, or attempts to bypass access and
playback controls.

Spoilers must use the spoiler control where available. Users can report content,
comments, accounts, and technical/copyright issues. Moderators may hide, remove,
restore, mute, suspend, or preserve material for investigation according to the
audit log and applicable law. Serious or repeated violations may terminate an
account. Provide an appeal/support path before publication.
