# Drovixa account deletion instructions

Publish this page at a stable HTTPS URL and link it from Google Play, the App
Store, Web, and the in-app Profile screen.

1. Sign in to Drovixa.
2. Open **Profile → Account & Security → Delete account**.
3. Review what will be deleted or retained for legal/security reasons.
4. Confirm identity and submit the deletion request.

If the user cannot access the account, provide a monitored request form or
email at `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`. Verify ownership without asking for passwords.
State the completion period, cancellation window, backup expiry period, and
records retained for fraud, tax, transaction, dispute, or legal obligations.
